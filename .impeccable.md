## Design Context

### Users

Three audiences, weighted toward the first:

1. **Recruiters and hiring managers (primary).** Arrive from a LinkedIn DM, an application
   portal, or a forwarded link. They are skimming a shortlist, often on mobile, often with
   several tabs open. They need identity, role, seniority, and location resolved in about
   two seconds. They are not reading; they are filtering.
2. **Prospective freelance clients.** Arrive from an email or Slack thread. They are
   evaluating capability and trustworthiness, and want a hint of what he actually builds.
3. **Peer developers and community.** Arrive from X, Reddit, or a shared link. They judge
   craft. Memorability and evident skill in the execution matter more than credentials.

**Job to be done:** convince a skimming stranger, in one glance, that this is a competent
engineer worth a second look — then make the deep detail (experience, projects, skills,
writing) easy to reach.

### Brand Personality

**Bold, kinetic, technical.**

- *Bold* — full-bleed, high-contrast, unafraid of scale. Type that commits rather than hedges.
- *Kinetic* — the site should feel like something in motion. Heat, drift, a beam that tracks
  your scroll. Energy is part of the identity, not decoration bolted on.
- *Technical* — the material vocabulary is engineering: monospace labels, hairline rules,
  precise alignment, a palette drawn from a shader's color stops.

Voice is first-person, plain, and unembellished. Short declarative sentences. No marketing
inflation, no "passionate about leveraging synergies." The work is the argument.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Theme: dark, and correctly so.** The site is consumed at night, on a laptop or phone, by
someone reviewing candidates or evaluating a build partner. A dark canvas makes the heat
palette glow and matches the shader that defines the hero. This is a derived choice, not a
default.

**Palette.** Near-black `#141414` field, off-white `#f5f5f5` type, with three accents lifted
directly from the `WoeHeatmap` shader: red `#dd0303` (primary), yellow `#fbff1a` (hot core),
blue `#1472ff` (cold edge). Red is the identity accent and must stay rare — roughly 10% of
visual weight. Yellow and blue appear only where heat is literally being depicted.

**Typography.** Three roles, never blurred:
- `Goldman` — display. Headings and the name. Geometric, engineered, slightly alien.
- `Sansation` — body. Clean, slightly technical, not the Inter monoculture.
- `Share Tech Mono` — labels, eyebrows, metadata, timestamps. Uppercase, wide tracking.

**Signature devices.**
- The **heat shader** (`logo.webp` through a Paper Design heatmap): a hard-edged monogram
  dissolving into a red/yellow/blue heat field. This is the single most recognizable asset.
- The **eyebrow label**: `font-mono text-xs tracking-[0.3em] text-accent`, uppercase, above
  every section heading. It is the site's most repeated structural motif.
- The **section rhythm**: `max-w-3xl`, `border-t border-border`, generous `py-24 md:py-32`.
- The **tracing beam**: a scroll-linked gradient stroke marking progress through experience.

**Anti-references.**
- Generic SaaS gradient cards: purple-to-blue wash, floating laptop mockup, soft drop shadow,
  centered logo. This is the single most important thing to avoid.
- Busy portfolio collages: screenshot grids, skill-badge soup, competing type sizes.
- Dark-neon developer cliché: terminal window chrome, blinking cursor, green-on-black,
  falling code. Dark does not mean cyberpunk.

### Design Principles

1. **Identity in two seconds.** Name, role, and location are resolved before any decoration
   is parsed. If a viewer has to hunt for who this is, the composition has failed.
2. **Accent is a scarcity economy.** Red earns attention precisely because it is rare. Every
   additional red element devalues the ones already there.
3. **Type does the work.** Hierarchy comes from scale contrast and weight, not from boxes,
   borders, or shadows. Aim for at least a 1.25 ratio between steps.
4. **On-system or absent.** New artifacts must look like a crop of the existing site — same
   tokens, same fonts, same eyebrow motif. No parallel aesthetic, no one-off styling.
5. **Motion implies life, never noise.** Kinetic energy comes from one or two orchestrated
   gestures, and every one of them respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
